# Notification center

Informational `OnLogMessage(..., bNotify=true)` events appear in the non-blocking Notifications tab instead of requiring a modal interruption. The tab offers local history, extended multi-selection, dismiss-selected, dismiss-all, clear-history, plain search, an opt-in bounded Qt regular-expression builder, and JSON/Markdown export of the active filtered view. Decision prompts remain modal because they require an answer.
